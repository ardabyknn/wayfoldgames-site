Support

Wayfold Games · Paint Train

Something not working, or an idea for the game? Write to us at support@wayfoldgames.com. Please tell us your phone model, the game version (shown at the bottom of the game's settings) and, if it is about a level, the level number. We usually answer within a few days.

Common questions

I bought Remove Ads (or the Starter Pack) but it is gone on my new phone. Open the game's settings and tap Restore. Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it.

I lost my progress. Progress is saved only on your phone, so it is lost when the game is deleted. We cannot restore it from our side.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Open the game's settings and tap Ad Privacy (shown in regions where the law asks for it). On iOS you can also allow or stop tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

How do I turn off reminders? In your phone's settings, open the game's notification settings and switch them off.

A level seems impossible. Every level is checked by a solver and can be won within its move limit. Try Undo, a Hint, or the Spare Track.
