Privacy Policy

Wayfold Games · Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what data our games collect and why. It applies to Paint Train and every other game published by Wayfold Games ("we", "us").

Short version

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • Our games show ads (Google AdMob) and measure gameplay (GameAnalytics). These partners collect device data as described below.
  • You can change your ad consent at any time in the game's settings.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.

1. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, address, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location (other than the approximate country an IP address points to), or payment card details.

2. Data stored on your device

Game progress (levels completed, coins, hints, settings such as sound, vibration and language) is stored locally on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes this data.

3. Advertising: Google AdMob

Our games show ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers (such as the advertising ID / IDFA, only if you allow it),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language),
  • ad interactions (ads shown, tapped, rewarded ads watched).

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch a Google consent form asks whether ads may be personalized. You can change this any time from the game's settings (Privacy options).
  • iOS: the app asks for permission before using the advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized using that identifier.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Analytics: GameAnalytics

We use GameAnalytics to understand how the game is played (for example, which levels are too hard, how long sessions last, when the game crashes). GameAnalytics may collect a random device/user ID, device and OS information, country, session times and in-game events (level start, complete, fail, booster and ad use). We use this only to improve our games.

GameAnalytics' policy: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy

5. In-app purchases

Purchases (such as Remove Ads, the Starter Pack or coin packs) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive your name, card or billing details. The game keeps a record of what you own (and the store's transaction ID, so a purchase is not delivered twice) on your device, and asks the store again when you tap Restore Purchases.

6. Notifications

If you allow it, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example, when a daily reward is ready). They are created on the device itself; no push service and no notification token are used. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

7. This website

wayfoldgames.com uses no cookies, no analytics and no ads. The demo level on the home page keeps its coins, achievements and sound setting in your browser's local storage; they never leave your browser and you can delete them by clearing the site's data. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Children

Our games are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data retention and deletion

We do not hold your data on our own servers. Data collected by Google and GameAnalytics is kept according to their policies. To ask for deletion, contact us with your request; we will forward it to the relevant partner. You can also reset your advertising ID on your device at any time.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Contact us to use these rights.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be noted on this page.

12. Contact

Wayfold Games Email: support@wayfoldgames.com
